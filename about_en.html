The Philosophy

I believe survival games have lost something along the way. They've become stressful, demanding, punitive.

With TerraBloom, I want to get back to basics: the satisfaction of building something that lasts.

  • No Crunch: The game is developed at a human pace, for humans.
  • No FOMO: You play when you want. The world waits for you.
  • Quality > Quantity: I prefer three mechanics polished to perfection than a hundred buggy features.
"A game should be a refuge, not a second to-do list."
