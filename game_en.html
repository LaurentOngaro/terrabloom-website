The Game

A unique blend of Action, Tower Defense, and Simulation.

The Core Loop

TerraBloom is built around a satisfying cycle of preparation, action, and reward. You are an Eco-Engineer sent to a devastated planet. Your mission: reactivate the Terraforming Cores.

  • Explore: Scout the area, find resources, and locate the dormant Core.
  • Build: Set up your base around the Core. Walls, turrets, energy generators.
  • Defend: Activate the Core. This will attract the "Blight", creatures born from pollution.
  • Restore: If you survive, the Core releases a purification wave, restoring the biome.

The Threat: The Blight

The planet is not empty. It is infested by the Blight, a semi-organic corruption that aggressively reacts to purification energy.

Enemies are not just mindless zombies. They evolve. They adapt to your defenses. Some will try to breach your walls, others will bombard you from afar, or disable your turrets.

Progression & Tech Tree

Between missions, return to your Orbital Station. Use the data collected to research new technologies.

  • Unlock new defensive structures (Laser Turrets, Shields, Traps).
  • Upgrade your Engineer's suit (Jetpack, Multi-tool, Health).
  • Discover new terraforming methods to access more difficult biomes.
