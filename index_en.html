TerraBloom

Build, Defend, Restore

A cozy action-defense game where you restore a dying world, zone by zone.

Discover Gameplay Stay Updated

The 3 Gameplay Phases

🏗️

Build

Create your base. Place walls, turrets, and resource collectors. Optimize your layout to withstand the assault.

🛡️

Defend

Survive waves of enemies corrupted by pollution. Use your weapons and defenses to protect the Core.

🌿

Restore

Use the energy collected to purify the land. Watch nature reclaim its rights and the biome transform.

A Living World

Every action has a visible impact.

In TerraBloom, you don't just fight enemies. You fight against desolation. Start in a gray, barren wasteland and transform it into a lush, vibrant paradise.

Explore varied biomes: from arid deserts to toxic swamps, each with its own challenges and unique flora.
