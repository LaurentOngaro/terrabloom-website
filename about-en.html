About TerraBloom

The Concept

TerraBloom is an action-defense game that fuses strategic construction, exploration, and intense combat sequences. Players assemble modular terrain elements, plan their defenses, and confront waves of enemies to protect an artifact that enables biome restoration. This three-phase structure alternates between tactical moments and action-packed sequences.

The Gameplay

The player explores a world divided into thematic chunks. Each chunk represents a distinct biome with unique restoration challenges: Desert Meadow (sandy lands blooming with life), Volcanic Jungle (lava flows transforming into verdant forests), Frozen Spring (ice melting into fresh waters), and Toxic Paradise (corrupted zones purified into vibrant ecosystems).

The game cycle is divided into three distinct phases:

  • Phase 0 - World Assembly (5-10 min): Assemble terrain with modular 3D chunks, create traversal paths, and position your defense towers.
  • Phase 1 - Strategic Exploration (7-15 min): Explore the environment while carrying the restoration artifact. Traverse your terrain and analyze optimal defensive positions.
  • Phase 2 - Intensive Defense (10-15 min): Defend the artifact against waves of hostile creatures and corrupted machines in FPS/TPS combat. Use upgradable weapons and skills. Survive all waves to secure biome restoration.

Outcome: The biome transforms (corrupted → vibrant), becomes a safe and preserved zone, and unlocks new areas. The loop repeats with increased complexity.

The Universe

TerraBloom takes place in a distant, dystopian future where Earth has become uninhabitable due to human-caused collapse (pollution, war, environmental destruction). Survivors retreated underground for generations, preserving knowledge and technology.

The Restoration Project uses advanced nano-tech artifact technology enabling planetary restoration zone by zone. Each artifact purifies soil, water, and air, stimulating rapid flora regrowth. However, the planet's evolved fauna and corrupted machines have become hyper-aggressive, and each restoration triggers waves of hostile entities.

Tone: Hopeful despite decay. Beauty persists even in corruption. Restoration is achievable—not grimdark despair.

Development

TerraBloom has been in development since December 2025 and is expected to launch between 2027 and 2028. The project follows a clear roadmap with several development phases:

  • Phase 1 (2026): Core prototype, foundational mechanics
  • Phase 2 (2026-2027): Game content, biomes, FPS/TD gameplay
  • Phase 3 (2027-2028): Polish, optimization and launch

Target Platform

TerraBloom is designed to run on a wide range of hardware, from mid-range PCs to current-generation consoles. The focus is on performance and optimization.

The Team

TerraBloom is developed by Laurent Ongaro, a software engineer with over 30 years of development experience. The project benefits from proven professional methods and technical expertise acquired over the years in varied environments, applied here to creating an ambitious indie game.
