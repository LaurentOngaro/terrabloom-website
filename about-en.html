About TerraBloom

The Concept

TerraBloom is an action-defense game that fuses strategic construction, exploration, and intense combat sequences. Players assemble modular terrain elements, plan their defenses, and confront waves of enemies to protect an artifact that enables biome restoration. This three-phase structure alternates between tactical moments and action-packed sequences.

Discover the Gameplay

TerraBloom offers a unique gameplay cycle where every decision impacts your survival.
The world is divided into thematic biomes, each offering specific challenges and opportunities.

📐 Phase 0: Crafting

Assemble your terrain with predefined terrain blocks. Create a passable path and plan your defense lines before the action begins.

🛡️ Phase 1: Strategy

Analyze the map and position your defense elements in strategic locations. Transport the restoration artifact to its installation point.

⚔️ Phase 2: Combat

Defend the artifact against waves of hostile creatures. Survive until the end of the cycle to restore life in the biome.

The "Chunk System": Infinite Replayability

Unlike classic terrains, TerraBloom uses an architecture based on Chunks (modular terrain blocks). Each session is different: you never play on the same map. You decide the world's topography, using the terrain to your advantage to optimize your defense.

Outcome: Once enemy waves are defeated, the restored biome becomes a safe zone that unlocks new technologies and new areas to explore.

The Universe

TerraBloom takes place in a distant, dystopian future where Earth has become uninhabitable due to human-caused collapse (pollution, war, environmental destruction). Survivors retreated underground for generations, preserving knowledge and technology.

The Restoration Project uses advanced nano-tech artifact technology enabling planetary restoration zone by zone. Each artifact purifies soil, water, and air, stimulating rapid flora regrowth. However, the planet's evolved fauna and corrupted machines have become hyper-aggressive, and each restoration triggers waves of hostile entities.

Tone: Hopeful despite decay. Beauty persists even in corruption. Restoration is achievable—not grimdark despair.
