About TerraBloom

The Concept

TerraBloom is an indie 3D exploration and defense game developed solo. The game combines building, defense, and restoration mechanics in a procedurally generated universe.

The Gameplay

The player explores a world divided into thematic chunks. Each chunk represents a distinct biome, ranging from frozen glaciers to desert lands, passing through corrupted zones and lush forests.

The game cycle is divided into three phases:

  • Build: Construct structures to progress and restore
  • Defend: Fight corrupted creatures and protect your progress
  • Restore: Bring nature back to life and transform lands

The Universe

TerraBloom takes place in a universe where nature has been corrupted by a dark force. As a restorer, your mission is to traverse this world and bring life back to devastated lands. Each biome has its own challenges, creatures, and restoration opportunities.

Development

TerraBloom has been in development since December 2025 and is expected to launch between 2027 and 2028. The project follows a clear roadmap with several development phases:

  • Phase 1 (2026): Core prototype, foundational mechanics
  • Phase 2 (2026-2027): Game content, biomes, FPS/TD gameplay
  • Phase 3 (2027-2028): Polish, optimization and launch

Target Platform

TerraBloom is designed to run on a wide range of hardware, from mid-range PCs to current-generation consoles. The focus is on performance and optimization.

The Team

TerraBloom was initially developed solo by Laurent Ongaro, Software engineer with over 30 years of programming experience with a strong interest in emerging technologies and video game creation, the project will be developed with expertise and professional rigor acquired over the years in a wide variety of technical environments.
