Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 6, 2026

1. Introduction

This privacy policy describes how TerraBloom collects, uses, and protects your personal data. We take your privacy seriously and are committed to complying with GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation).

2. Data We Collect

We only collect data that you voluntarily provide:

  • Contact information: Name and email address (via contact form)
  • Message content: Information you choose to include in your messages
  • Analytics data: Anonymized browsing information (pages visited, duration, device type) via Google Analytics

3. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to:

  • Respond to your questions and requests
  • Improve site content and user experience
  • Analyze site usage anonymously

4. Legal Basis (GDPR)

Data processing is based on:

  • Your explicit consent when using the contact form
  • Legitimate interest for anonymized traffic analysis

5. Your Rights

Under GDPR, you have the following rights:

  • Right of access: Request a copy of your data
  • Right to rectification: Correct your data
  • Right to erasure: Request deletion of your data
  • Right to data portability: Receive your data in a readable format
  • Right to object: Refuse data processing

To exercise these rights, contact us via the contact form.

6. Data Sharing

Your data is only shared with essential services:

Formspree (Contact Form)

  • GDPR-compliant service used to process contact form messages
  • Privacy policy: Formspree Privacy Policy

Google Analytics

  • Used to analyze traffic anonymously
  • Data is anonymized and cannot personally identify you

We never sell your data to third parties.

7. Data Security

  • HTTPS secure connection across the entire site
  • No local storage of personal data
  • Message processing via Formspree (GDPR-compliant servers)

8. Cookies

This site uses cookies strictly necessary for operation:

  • Theme preferences (light/dark)
  • Language preference (FR/EN)
  • Google Analytics (anonymized)

No advertising tracking or profiling cookies are used.

9. Data Retention

  • Contact messages: 1 year for communication archives
  • Analytics data: 26 months (Google Analytics policy)

10. Changes

This policy may be updated. Significant changes will be indicated on this page with a new update date.

11. Contact

For any questions regarding this policy or your personal data:

  • Use the contact form
  • Find all links on the About page
