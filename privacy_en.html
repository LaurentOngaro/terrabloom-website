Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to the TerraBloom website. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy will inform you as to how we look after your personal data when you visit our website.

2. Data We Collect

We currently collect minimal data:

  • Newsletter: If you sign up for our newsletter, we collect your email address. This is used solely to send you updates about the game.
  • Contact Form: If you contact us via the form, we receive your name, email, and message content.
  • Analytics: We may use anonymous, cookie-free analytics to understand website traffic (e.g., page views).

3. How We Use Your Data

We will only use your personal data when the law allows us to. Most commonly, we will use your personal data in the following circumstances:

  • To communicate with you (reply to inquiries).
  • To send you the newsletter you subscribed to.

4. Data Security

We have put in place appropriate security measures to prevent your personal data from being accidentally lost, used, or accessed in an unauthorized way.

5. Third-Party Links

This website may include links to third-party websites (e.g., Twitter, YouTube, Steam). Clicking on those links may allow third parties to collect or share data about you. We do not control these third-party websites and are not responsible for their privacy statements.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us via the Contact page.
